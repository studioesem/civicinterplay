The four registers

Fig. 2. Point 03, then 04. One each, then swap.

Register

Heart

What here would you defend, and from what?

  1. The attachment.
  2. The loss you can already name.
  3. Who else holds it.

Trap: feeling standing in for a claim.

Register

Body

What is your body registering that has not yet become words?

  1. The sense that dominated.
  2. The sense that was shut down.
  3. What you moved away from.

Trap: describing the view instead of the sensation.

Register

Mind

What structure do you think is operating here?

  1. The pattern.
  2. The counter-example.
  3. Who the pattern pays.

Trap: arriving with the answer already loaded.

Register

Agent

What would you set a machine looking for, and what will it miss?

  1. The question worth delegating.
  2. The context it cannot have.
  3. What you will check by hand afterwards.

Trap: opening the window before the other three have spoken.

The guides

Fig. 3. Nine plates. Keys for Margulis, Langton and Denes are drafts.

Ursula K. Le Guin. Source: The Marginalian.

Ursula Le Guin

Key: the bag

Terrain
What this place holds, rather than what it does.
The act
Name five things this place carries. Then one it refuses to carry.

Walter Benjamin. Source to confirm.

Walter Benjamin

Key: the fragment

Terrain
The outmoded and the discarded. The past lodged inside the present.
The act
Pick up one thrown-away thing. Read it as a document.

Elinor Ostrom. Source to confirm.

Elinor Ostrom

Key: the boundary

Terrain
Rules in use, as distinct from rules on paper. She and Vincent called their Bloomington centre a Workshop, not an institute.
The act
Draw the edge of one shared thing here. Then find out who watches it.

James Lovelock. Source to confirm.

James Lovelock

Key: the instrument

Terrain
Metabolism. Heat, water, air, load.
The act
If you could read only this place’s breath, how would you know it is alive?

Lynn Margulis. Source to confirm.

Lynn Margulis

Key: the merger

Terrain
What looks like one thing here and is two that can no longer be separated.
The act
Name a partnership wearing a single name. Say who is living inside whom.

Marcia Langton. Source to confirm.

Marcia Langton

Key: the agreement

Terrain
Whose country this is, and what has been negotiated on whose behalf.
The act
Name who speaks for this place, by what authority, and who was not at the table.

Agnes Denes, Rice/Tree/Burial, 1968. Artist’s documentation.

Agnes Denes

Key: the planting

Terrain
What would grow here if somebody insisted, and what the growing would confront.
The act
Choose the most valuable ground in sight. Say what you would plant on it.

Carlo Rovelli. Source to confirm.

Carlo Rovelli

Key: the interval

Terrain
The durations layered in one site. Tidal, budgetary, electoral, a training run.
The act
Name three clocks running here at different speeds. Say which one governs.

David Graeber. Source: The New York Times.

David Graeber

Key: the IOU

Terrain
Obligation. The arbitrary wearing the clothes of the necessary.
The act
Name one rule everybody here obeys that somebody made up.
