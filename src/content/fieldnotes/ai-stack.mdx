## The short version

Three vertical integrators, and one horizontal middle. Australia holds the ground that the stack is built on, and almost nothing above it. The layer granted through state planning is the layer where public notice is exempted, which means the one part of the stack under Australian control is also the part decided with the least public involvement.

Read the stack from the bottom up and it is a story about resources. Read it from the top down and it is a story about where the returns go. Both readings end in the same middle, and that middle is the only place a state government currently has leverage.

## What to do with this

The useful question is not who is to blame, it is where the leverage sits. Layer 02 is the answer, because it is the layer a state or local government actually decides. The companion field note sets out what could be asked for at that point of decision.
