This project is entirely speculative, and the information presented here is highly indicative. The diagram below presents a way of constructing an exploded axonometric view of a place through its historical public registers of investment, set against named priorities and targets.

What would happen if the flows of value and investment were made more legible in this way? The example below shows a city government's investment in tree canopy, and where misalignments between proposed and actual spending and infrastructure were evidenced.

Above all, the visualisation asks: what does it mean to reassemble public records in ways that make flows of value more intentionally explicit?
