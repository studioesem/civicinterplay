In 2018 Western Sydney was promised a parkland city. This drawing pulls one precinct apart to read that promise: what was said for the place on top, the approvals and spending in the middle, and the ground below. Click a numbered gap to go to its station on the walk. Drag the timeline to watch the record build from 2018, and past today to the targets the plans point to.
