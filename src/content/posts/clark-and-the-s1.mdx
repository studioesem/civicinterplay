On 18 July 2023 Jack Clark, an Anthropic co-founder, addressed the UN Security Council. When he posted his remarks, he named "the significant political power which AI development creates and how this leverage is currently accruing to private sector actors" as one of the "two central issues of AI in the 21st century". In November 2026 Anthropic filed Amendment No. 1 to its S-1 registration statement for its initial public offering. This page sets eleven passages from the speech next to passages from the filing.

**Sources.** Jack Clark, "AI Safety and Corporate Power – remarks given at the United Nations Security Council", jack-clark.net, 18 July 2023. Anthropic, PBC, Form S-1/A (Amendment No. 1), filed with the US Securities and Exchange Commission on 2 November 2026; page numbers are prospectus pages.

**Method.** Passages were selected by Sarah Barns, who shared with Claude to identify the extent to which the 'civic spirit' and concerns in Clark's speech had been upheld as the company sought to expand its corporate power. Each pairing was checked against both texts by independent AI skeptic agents on 7 November 2026: 10 of 11 confirmed, one page reference corrected. Claude is a model made by the company discussed here.

**Civic Spirit rating.** Each row asks whether the 2026 arrangement keeps the purpose behind the 2023 statement. The ratings were proposed by Sarah and then implemented by Claude, but not yet cross-checked by Sarah. What the ratings mean is summarised below.
