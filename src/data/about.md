## Open experimentation

This is a methodology that is in development, which means that experimentation is deliberately open. Not everything will be successful; perhaps some tools and ideas will be mistakes. The purpose behind this is to build a greater sense of agency among those who may feel excluded from decisions about how value is being captured and shared within their communities.

While transparency is never an end in itself, the methods here seek to bring attention to the important role that everyday digital capabilities play in shaping civic agency, towards more effective governance.

## On the use of AI

Agents and AI workflows are key to the civic interplay method. Agentic workflows are tested and trialled, in order to read and interrogate information records that are publicly available. This includes open government data and other information that is available via the public web. By diving in, these methods also establish practices of critical enquiry and sense-checking around the adoption of agents as effective co-enquirers. This practice also experimentally seeks to understand where the new forms of 'data enclosure' exist or are emerging, in the way that previous platform ecosystems built the moats around their intelligence. 

AI is not supported as a writing tool, wherever possible.
